    How the prediction works

    ScanWe watch it frame by frame

    Your browser samples the video every half second to measure motion, scene cuts, brightness, contrast, color and how hard the first 3 seconds hit. Audio is checked for loudness and dead air.

    ScoreEight signals, one score

    Hook, pacing, visuals, audio, length, format, caption and timing are weighted the way the Shorts and For You feeds reward them: retention first, everything else after.

    ForecastYour channel sets the baseline

    Subscribers and recent average views set where you start. The score moves the forecast up or down from there, and the range widens when we have less to go on.

    PreView is an estimate built from signals the algorithms are known to reward. It is not connected to YouTube or TikTok and can't see your real analytics, so treat the range as a guide. Short-form views are very uneven, and a video can land well outside it.
